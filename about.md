## Biography

Jecha S Jecha is a PhD student in Educational Technology at **Southwest
University** in Chongqing, China, and an Assistant Lecturer at **Zanzibar
University** in Tanzania. His doctoral research investigates how artificial
intelligence can support students in overcoming academic procrastination and
developing self-regulated learning strategies.

Before beginning his doctoral studies, Jecha taught undergraduate courses in
educational technology and research methods at Zanzibar University, where he
developed a lasting interest in the gap between students' intentions and
their actions — the everyday struggle with planning, starting, and finishing
academic work. That interest now drives a research program that combines
learning analytics, motivation theory, and human-AI interaction.

His current work centers on three connected questions:

1. **Why do students procrastinate?** Understanding the motivational,
   emotional, and environmental antecedents of academic procrastination in
   higher education.
2. **What can AI observe?** Using learning analytics and behavioral trace
   data to detect procrastination and self-regulation breakdowns as they
   happen.
3. **How should AI respond?** Designing AI-supported interventions and
   feedback systems that help learners regulate goals, time, and effort
   without undermining their autonomy.

Jecha's perspective is shaped by teaching and studying across two very
different higher-education contexts — East Africa and China — and he is
particularly interested in how educational AI can be designed for, and
evaluated in, low-resource settings.
